Laboratory research only

All products listed on this website are supplied for laboratory research use only. They are not medicines, medical devices, cosmetics, food, dietary supplements or diagnostic agents. They are not for human or veterinary use, and must not be administered to humans or animals.

No medical advice

Nothing on this website is medical advice, a prescription, a treatment recommendation or a dosing instruction. Product summaries describe research context and published or catalog information; they do not establish safety or efficacy for any human or veterinary use.

The purchaser is responsible for confirming that the material may be lawfully imported, possessed and used for laboratory research in their jurisdiction. Buyers must comply with applicable laws, import requirements and institutional approvals.

Public CAS numbers, formulas and molecular weights are reference data. The lot-specific certificate of analysis and the supplier's shipping documents govern the material actually supplied. Values may be unavailable or not applicable for blends, proteins and proprietary mixtures.
