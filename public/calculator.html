01 / FORMULAS

Every output is traceable to one line.

No hidden adjustment factors, no assumed dose, no recommended protocol. The ledger is a transparent unit-conversion surface.

FORMULAS USED

  • mass (mg) ÷ diluent (mL) = concentration (mg/mL)concentration
  • concentration × 100 = mcg per 0.1 mLsmall-volume check
  • target (mcg) ÷ (concentration × 1,000) = volume (mL)aliquot volume
  • volume (mL) × 100 = U-100 unitssyringe scale
  • total mass (mcg) ÷ target (mcg) = aliquotswhole-vial count

WORKED EXAMPLE

5 mg + 2 mL + 250 mcg target amount

Concentration5 ÷ 2 = 2.5 mg/mL
Amount in 0.1 mL2.5 × 100 = 250 mcg
Volume per 250 mcg250 ÷ 2,500 = 0.1 mL
U-100 scale0.1 × 100 = 10 U
Aliquots per vial5,000 ÷ 250 = 20

Read it as a ratio, not a regimen. Change the target amount and the volume changes; concentration stays fixed until mass or diluent changes.

Lyophilised powder

Typically frozen and desiccated, protected from light. Let the vial equilibrate before opening to reduce condensation.

Protect from light

Many peptides are light-sensitive. Use amber or foil-wrapped secondary containment where the protocol requires it.

Aliquot, don't repeat freeze–thaw

Repeated cycling can change aggregation and activity. Plan single-use aliquots where the material and protocol allow.

Use-by is lot-specific

Do not assume a universal beyond-use date. Ask for the lot data and follow the documented storage conditions.

02 / LEDGER QUESTIONS

Questions about the arithmetic.

Is concentration the same as a dose?

No. The ledger calculates concentration and aliquot volume from the values entered. It does not recommend an amount, frequency or route, and nothing here is a dosing instruction. The target amount must come from your own approved laboratory protocol.

Why show both mg/mL and micrograms per 0.1 mL?

Milligram-per-millilitre is the standard concentration expression. Micrograms per 0.1 mL is a convenient small-volume check when a protocol is written around a 0.1 mL aliquot. Both are derived from the same concentration and are shown together to make the conversion visible.

What if the calculated volume exceeds the syringe?

The U-100 readout flags volumes above 100 units. Use a larger syringe, split the measurement, or revisit the target amount and diluent volume with your protocol. Do not estimate past the graduations of the syringe you have.

Does the ledger account for dead volume?

No. It assumes complete dissolution and that the entered diluent volume is the final volume. Real vials have dead volume, needle holdup and sometimes powder displacement. Those losses are separate from the arithmetic and should be characterised in your own validation.

Can I send the calculation to the desk?

Yes. The WhatsApp, email and copy buttons include the mass, diluent volume, target amount, calculated concentration, aliquot volume and a link to the same calculation. Add your destination and quantity before sending.
