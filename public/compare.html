CJC-1295 with DAC vs without DAC

Growth axis

The core difference is pharmacokinetic, not receptor-level. Both are GHRH-receptor analogues; the DAC form carries a drug-affinity complex designed to bind albumin and extend exposure.

BPC-157 vs TB-500

Repair models

These are frequently discussed together but are not close chemical relatives. BPC-157 is a 15-amino-acid peptide; TB-500 is the 7-amino-acid thymosin β4 fragment 17-23 (Ac-SDKP).

Comparator FAQ

Questions about comparing.

What should I compare first: price or identity?

Identity. A lower price is not a useful comparison if the sequence, salt form, fill or documentation differs. Start with CAS or sequence, molecular weight and the exact pack format, then compare price per pack or per vial.

Can I compare a peptide with a blend?

Yes, but the comparison is asymmetric. A blend has multiple active components and a fixed ratio; a single peptide has one. Compare the blend's disclosed composition and ratio with the individual components rather than treating the total milligram fill as equivalent.

Why is no molecular weight shown for some entries?

Blends, heterogeneous preparations, recombinant proteins and supplier-coded items do not have one meaningful molecular formula or weight. The page states that rather than inventing a value. Ask for the sequence or composition on the lot CoA.

Can I send the comparison directly for a quote?

Yes. The WhatsApp, email and copy buttons include the selected entries, their CAS or identity status, the list price shown and a link back to the comparison. Add your destination and quantity in the message before sending.

Does the comparator recommend a product?

No. It structures published identity and research-context data for laboratory evaluation. It does not recommend human use, provide dosing, or claim that two products are interchangeable.
