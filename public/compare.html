GROWTH AXIS / PHARMACOKINETICS

CJC-1295 with DAC vs without DAC

The core difference is pharmacokinetic, not receptor-level. Both are GHRH-receptor analogues; the DAC form carries a drug-affinity complex designed to bind albumin and extend exposure.

FIELDWITH DACWITHOUT DAC
Identity29-aa analogue plus maleimidopropionic-acid DAC29-aa modified analogue; no DAC
TargetGHRH receptorGHRH receptor
ExposureAlbumin binding supports a half-life measured in daysRapid clearance; half-life on the order of minutes
From$135.80 / pack$311.65 / pack

Readout: choose the DAC form for extended exposure and the no-DAC form for a short pulse. Confirm the DAC modification and sequence on the lot file; do not substitute by mass.

02

REPAIR MODELS / IDENTITY

BPC-157 vs TB-500

These are frequently discussed together but are not close chemical relatives. BPC-157 is a 15-amino-acid peptide; TB-500 is the 7-amino-acid thymosin β4 fragment 17-23 (Ac-SDKP).

FIELDBPC-157TB-500
Length15 amino acids7 amino acids, N-acetylated
Molecular weight1,419.5 Da889 Da
Research focusGI protection, angiogenesis, cytoprotectionActin dynamics, cell migration, matrix remodelling
From$67.70 / pack$100.90 / pack

Readout: not interchangeable. A 1:1 blend is a supplier convenience format, not evidence of synergy. Use separate records when the experiment needs independent concentration control.

FORMAT CHOICE

BPC-157 + TB-500 blend vs separate records

A fixed blend saves a preparation step but removes independent control of each component. The 1:1 ratio is a catalog format, not a validated pharmacological ratio.

03 / METHOD NOTE

What the readout does — and does not do.

It compares

Identity, target, structure, half-life, evidence, pack schedules and indicative list prices. Missing public data is labeled rather than guessed.

It does not

Recommend human use, calculate a dose, claim equivalence or replace a lot file. The preparation ledger is a separate arithmetic tool.

04 / READOUT QUESTIONS

Before you compare.

What should I compare first?

Identity and format. A lower price is not useful if the sequence, salt form, fill or document set differs. Start with CAS or sequence, molecular weight and the exact pack schedule, then compare price per pack or per vial.

Can a blend be compared with a single peptide?

Yes, but the comparison is asymmetric. A blend has multiple components and a fixed ratio; a single peptide has one. Compare the blend's disclosed composition and ratio with the individual components rather than treating total milligram fill as equivalent.

Why is no molecular weight shown for some records?

Blends, heterogeneous preparations, recombinant proteins and supplier-coded items do not have one meaningful formula or weight. The record states that instead of inventing a value. Ask for the sequence or composition on the lot file.

Can I send the comparison as a quotation request?

Yes. The WhatsApp, email and copy buttons include the selected records, identity status, the list price shown and a link to the comparison. Add your destination and quantity before sending.

Does the comparator recommend a material?

No. It structures identity and research-context data for laboratory evaluation. It does not recommend human use, provide dosing, or claim that two materials are interchangeable.
