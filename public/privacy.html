Privacy

A short privacy notice.

This site is a static register and quotation aid. It does not run third-party analytics, advertising pixels or marketing cookies. Contact happens through your own email or WhatsApp app.

No marketing cookies Effective 27 November 2026

What this site collects

The website itself does not provide an account area, checkout or live database. The calculator runs entirely in your browser and does not transmit the values you enter. The contact form composes a message in your email or WhatsApp app; it does not submit to a server operated by this site.

Server logs

The site is delivered through a hosting and CDN provider, which may process standard request information (such as IP address, user agent and requested URL) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Those logs are controlled by the hosting provider's terms and retention settings.

When you contact us

If you email or message us, we receive the information you choose to send: your name, company, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, destination and product inquiry. We use it to respond to the inquiry and prepare a quotation. Do not send medical records, government identifiers or other sensitive personal data.

Sharing

We do not sell contact data. Information may be shared with service providers only where needed to answer the inquiry, arrange logistics or comply with law. We do not use the contact form to subscribe you to a marketing list.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the contact information associated with a quotation by emailing info@weike.com. If you have already sent a message through WhatsApp or email, that provider's privacy terms also apply.

External links

Pages may link to PubChem, payment or logistics providers, or other third-party sites. Those sites have their own privacy practices. This notice does not cover them.
