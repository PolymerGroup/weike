Why this reference exists

Most sourcing mistakes happen before the price is discussed.

Research buyers are often shown a name, a milligram fill and a number. What is missing is the part that determines whether the material is the right one: sequence or CAS, salt form, receptor context, pack configuration and the lot document that will travel with it.

This site is structured around those questions. The encyclopedia gives every entry the same fields. The comparator exposes differences instead of hiding them behind a “best seller” label. The calculator keeps preparation arithmetic transparent. The quotation process then confirms the exact material and destination requirements.

We work with research groups, clinics, distributors and laboratory procurement teams that need a documented starting point — not a promise that every peptide is interchangeable.

Operating principles

What we optimise for.

The commercial process is deliberately downstream of identity and documentation. That is slower than a one-click catalog, and more useful for a research purchase.

Identity is a gate, not a detail

We show CAS, formula and molecular weight where a public record exists, and we say when it does not. A sequence or lot identity should be settled before price.

Documentation before dispatch

A useful CoA identifies the lot, the analytical method and the result. We ask buyers to review it rather than treating a website badge as proof.

Small batch or distribution schedule

A single research group and a distributor need different quotation formats. The same reference structure supports both, with quantities and destination confirmed in writing.

No therapeutic claims

The catalog is not a clinic. We do not provide human or veterinary dosing, treatment advice or claims of clinical benefit.

How an inquiry is handled

Four checkpoints before a quotation leaves.

Send a product, format and destination to start. If the request is incomplete, we will ask for the missing detail rather than assume it.

01 / 04

Specification review

We check the requested name, format, quantity and destination against the catalog entry. Ambiguous names are clarified before a price is issued.

02 / 04

Documentation check

We confirm which lot documents are available for the material under discussion. The buyer receives the document set before committing.

03 / 04

Destination review

Freight, packaging and import documentation are reviewed for the destination country. Regulated items receive additional scrutiny.

04 / 04

Written quotation

The quotation states the material, pack format, price basis, exclusions and next steps. No hidden substitution.

Boundaries

What we will not claim.

Certification we do not hold

We do not display ISO, GMP, FDA or third-party certification seals unless the specific facility or lot documentation supports the claim. A visual badge is not a substitute for a certificate.

Human or veterinary use

We do not sell these materials as medicines, cosmetics or supplements and do not provide dosing. The research-use-only restriction is the boundary of the catalog.

Silent substitution

If a format, salt or documentation set differs from the request, it should be stated before the order proceeds. The buyer should be able to compare the CoA with the order.

Invented identity data

If the source catalog does not publish a CAS or formula, this site labels the gap. We do not fill it with a plausible-looking number.
