A better first message

Four details that speed up a quote.

Incomplete inquiries create avoidable back-and-forth. These four fields let us check the exact material and destination without guessing.

01

Material

Name, CAS or sequence, and the exact catalog entry if you have one. For blends, state the ratio.

02

Format

Milligram or IU fill, vials per pack, and quantity. A price without a format is not comparable.

03

Destination

Country and, if relevant, the import route. Freight and documentation depend on the destination.

04

Documentation

Lot-specific CoA, SDS or both. State the identity and purity methods your lab needs to review.

Use restriction. All quotations and products are for laboratory research use only. We do not provide medical advice, human dosing, veterinary use or compounding guidance.
