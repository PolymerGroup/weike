Handling & storage

Lyophilised powder: -20 °C, protected from light and moisture. Reconstituted: 2–8 °C, use within the period supported by the lot documentation. Avoid repeated freeze–thaw.

  • Receive. Inspect the vial, cap, label and lot number. Photograph any damage before opening.
  • Lyophilised storage. Keep the powder frozen and desiccated, protected from light. Equilibrate sealed vials before opening to limit condensation.
  • After reconstitution. Store at 2–8 °C for the period supported by the lot documentation. Do not assume a universal beyond-use date.
  • Handling. Avoid repeated freeze–thaw and vigorous mixing. Use single-use aliquots where the protocol permits.
