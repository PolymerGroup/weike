INDEX NOTES

How the register is assembled.

A reference layer over a supplier list, not a substitute for a lot file.

Why are several catalog labels merged into one record?

A supplier list may use different labels for the same material. The register normalizes those variants into one canonical record so that identity data and pack schedules are reviewed together. Every source label remains mapped in the downloadable coverage sheet.

Why is a CAS number missing from some records?

The source list does not include CAS numbers or formulas. We add a public structure record only where a confident match exists. Blends, proteins, proprietary mixtures and supplier-coded items do not have a single reliable CAS; those records state that directly and point to the lot file.

How does the research-family filter work?

The filter uses broad receptor or research families such as incretin and amylin, GH axis, repair and actin, copper and matrix, immune signalling, metabolic and mitochondrial, neuro and opioid, longevity and redox, melanocortin, and reproductive endocrine. Keyword search also covers names, aliases, CAS numbers and target text.

Are these the final prices?

No. The register shows indicative USD list prices per pack for planning and comparison. A quotation confirms current availability, exact fill, documentation, freight and destination requirements. Prices exclude freight, duties, taxes and bank charges.

What if a pack schedule is unavailable?

Availability can vary by material and destination. Send the exact schedule and quantity. If it is unavailable, we will say so and identify the nearest documented format rather than silently substituting a different fill or salt form.
