02

Mechanism & structure

VIP activates VPAC receptors and increases cAMP, influencing vasodilation, secretion and neuroimmune signalling. It is a large, structured neuropeptide.

Primary research target

VPAC1 / VPAC2 receptors; PAC1-related signalling

Structure / identity

28-amino-acid linear peptide with a C-terminal amide; sequence should match the lot CoA.

Half-life and exposure

Short; rapid proteolytic degradation.

03

Research file

04

Comparison note

VIP is much larger than short neuropeptides such as Selank. It acts through VPAC receptors, not GABA or melanocortin receptors.

Handling note

Lyophilised powder: -20 °C, protected from light and moisture. Reconstituted: 2–8 °C, use within the period supported by the lot documentation. Avoid repeated freeze–thaw.

Receive

Inspect the vial, cap, label and lot number. Photograph any damage before opening.

Lyophilised storage

Keep the powder frozen and desiccated, protected from light. Equilibrate sealed vials before opening to limit condensation.

After reconstitution

Store at 2–8 °C for the period supported by the lot documentation. Do not assume a universal beyond-use date.

Handling

Avoid repeated freeze–thaw and vigorous mixing. Use single-use aliquots where the protocol permits.
