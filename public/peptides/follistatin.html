What is Follistatin-344?

Follistatin-344 is a recombinant glycoprotein that binds activin and myostatin. It is studied for muscle-growth and reproductive biology, not as a small peptide.

Research classGrowth & Secretagogues
EvidencePreclinical
Catalog formats1 listed

Evidence context. Published data are mainly in vitro or animal models; human evidence is not established.

Mechanism & structural notes

Follistatin sequesters activin and myostatin, reducing their receptor-mediated signalling. This can affect muscle mass and reproductive endpoints in animal models.

Primary research target

Activin A, myostatin (GDF-8) and related TGF-β-family ligands

Structure / identity

Glycosylated protein with multiple domains (~31–39 kDa depending on isoform); not a synthetic short peptide.

Half-life and exposure

Protein-dependent; route and glycosylation affect exposure.

Research context

The following are research-model areas associated with this material in published or catalog literature. They are not approved indications and do not establish safety or efficacy in humans.

  • Muscle-growth models
  • Myostatin/activin signalling
  • Reproductive biology

How it compares

Follistatin binds myostatin extracellularly, while GDF-8 is the ligand itself. They are not interchangeable research tools.
