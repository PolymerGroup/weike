What is Bacteriostatic Water?

Bacteriostatic water is sterile water containing 0.9% benzyl alcohol as a preservative, used for reconstitution of lyophilised laboratory materials.

Research classLab Supplies & Diluents
EvidenceLaboratory supply
Catalog formats2 listed

Evidence context. A non-peptide reagent or diluent. Handle according to its safety data sheet.

Mechanism & structural notes

Benzyl alcohol suppresses microbial growth; the water acts as the solvent. It is not a research peptide and is not for human or veterinary injection.

Frequently asked questions

Is bacteriostatic water the same as sterile water?

No. It contains 0.9% benzyl alcohol as a preservative. That can be useful for multi-draw laboratory work but may interfere with some assays. Sterile water without preservative is preferable for certain applications.

Is this product for injection?

No. It is a laboratory diluent supplied for research use only and is not for human or veterinary injection.
