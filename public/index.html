What is different here

A sourcing record, not a sales brochure.

The reference is designed for the questions that arrive before a purchase order: what exactly is it, what format is quoted, and what document will accompany the lot?

Identity before price

CAS, formula and molecular weight are shown where a public structure record exists. Where the source catalog is silent, the page says so instead of filling the gap.

Pack-level clarity

Every listed format is priced per pack with a per-vial figure where the pack count is known. No hidden 'starting at' unit switch.

A document trail

Each entry links to handling notes, CoA guidance and a written inquiry format. Ask for the lot-specific document set before you commit.

Two buyer paths

Small clinics and labs can request a single-pack quote. Distributors can send a multi-line schedule for a consolidated quotation.

01 / 03

Define the material

Start with the exact peptide, sequence or CAS, pack format and quantity. If the catalog name is ambiguous, request the sequence before comparing prices.

02 / 03

Verify the documentation

Ask for the lot-specific CoA, identity method, purity method and net content. Treat a generic certificate as a starting point, not a substitute for lot data.

03 / 03

Quote the destination

Send the destination country and any import requirements with the inquiry. Freight, documentation and availability are confirmed against that destination.

Before you inquire

Questions worth asking first.

Short answers on use restrictions, pricing, documentation and dispatch. For product-specific questions, open the entry or send a brief.

Are these products for human use?

No. Every product in this catalog is supplied for laboratory research use only. It is not for human or veterinary use, diagnosis, treatment, compounding, cosmetic application or food use. The site does not provide medical advice or dosing instructions.

How are the list prices shown on the site determined?

Prices are indicative USD list prices per catalog pack, shown to help with early-stage planning. A formal quotation confirms the current pack format, availability, documentation and freight to your destination. List prices exclude freight, duties, taxes and bank charges.

Can I request a lot-specific certificate of analysis?

Yes. Ask for the lot-specific CoA before purchase and review identity, purity method, net content, appearance and storage instructions. We do not publish blanket purity claims that are not tied to a specific lot.

Do you ship outside China?

We quote international freight from Jinan. Destination-specific import requirements and documentation are reviewed before dispatch; the buyer is responsible for confirming that the material may be lawfully imported for laboratory research.

What is the minimum order?

Minimum order quantities depend on the pack format, product and destination. They are confirmed in the quotation rather than assumed on the website. Small research groups and distributors can both send a specification for review.
